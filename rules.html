HELLO Who's Who

Guess the teammate behind the answers

Everyone answers a few questions in secret. Then a mystery teammate's answers come out one clue at a time, and you race to work out who it is. Takes about 20 minutes for a team of seven.

Before the game

  1. Open the game link on your phone or laptop. No account, nothing to install.
  2. Fill in your card. Your name plus at least five answers. Keep answers short, and don't write anything that names you.
  3. Tap "Put me on the board". Your tile appears on the shared screen.

One person shares their screen and runs the game. They don't guess, but they can still be a mystery person.

Each round

  1. A clue appears on the shared screen and on your phone. Either/or answers come first, the telling ones later.
  2. Flip down the people it can't be by tapping their tiles.
  3. Lock in your guess when you dare: switch to "Guess", tap a tile, confirm. You get one shot and no take-backs.
  4. Reveal. Every tile drops except the mystery person, and you see who guessed whom.

Points

The earlier you lock in, the more a correct guess is worth.

  • When it's your own card on screen, you don't guess. Keep a poker face.
  • Most points after everyone has been the mystery person wins.
